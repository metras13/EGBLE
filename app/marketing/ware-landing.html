Simple by Design

Tap the Orb. Set the Glow.

The whole app centers on one control: a glowing orb. Tap it to turn your lights on or off, drag to dim. Pick a preset and it applies instantly to every zone, no menus, no learning curve.

Wear mode strips the app down to just what you need while you are out: the orb, activities, power, and safety. Flip to Store mode for the full pattern editor and speed controls.

Light control that fits in a pocket and works with a glance.

Everything you set runs on the controller itself, so your lighting keeps going even if your phone locks or wanders out of range. Reconnect and the app shows you exactly what the lights are doing right now.

Power That Lasts

Bright Now, or All Night

Trade brightness for runtime with one tap. When your swap battery runs low, Ware nudges you to Endurance so you are never left in the dark.

Endurance

Dimmed to stretch a single charge across the whole night. Ware suggests it automatically when the battery drops.

Built by Ellumiglow

15 Years of Lighting Experience

Ware is built by Ellumiglow, a Portland-based lighting technology company with over 15 years of experience engineering light as a material. From electroluminescent panels on live entertainment stages to wearable safety lighting, we treat light as something that should work for people, not just look good.

Light as a material. Not an afterthought.

Ware comes from the same philosophy that drives all of our products: light should be intentional, integrated, and designed for the context it is used in. What you wear at night deserves the same attention that professional lighting designers bring to the stage.

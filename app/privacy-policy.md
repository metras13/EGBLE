# Ware Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Ware ("the app") is a Bluetooth companion app for the Ware EL light controller.
This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

## Information we collect

None. Ware does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There
are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

## Bluetooth

Ware uses Bluetooth solely to discover and control your own Ware controller. The
Bluetooth connection carries only lighting commands and controller status
between your phone and your controller. Ware does not send any of this data to us
or to any third party.

## Location

On Android, the operating system requires the location permission in order to
scan for Bluetooth devices. Ware does not use, store, or share your location. On
iOS, no location permission is requested.

## Data storage

Your app preferences (such as brightness and your chosen number of channels) are
stored only on your device. They are never uploaded.

## Children

Ware does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new
"last updated" date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support contact listed on the
Ware App Store page.
